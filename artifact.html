Kente Cloth

Origin: Ghana (Ashanti/Ewe peoples)

Material: Hand-woven silk and cotton

Linguistic significance: Patterns encode proverbs and historical narratives in Twi and Ewe

Adinkra Symbols

Origin: Ghana (Akan people)

Material: Clay stamps on fabric or pottery

Linguistic significance: Each symbol carries a Twi proverb with deep philosophical meaning

Traditional Masks

Origin: Various West African regions

Material: Wood, clay, textiles

Linguistic significance: Used in masquerade traditions that combine performance, poetry, and language
