The problem

A language disappears somewhere in the world every two weeks.

!

Languages are fading

Across Africa, young people are growing up unable to speak their grandparents' tongues. When a language dies, its stories, proverbs and knowledge die with it.

!

School leaves them out

UNESCO estimates that 40% of people worldwide lack access to education in a language they actually speak or understand. Indigenous languages are rarely taught at all.

!

Apps ignore them

The big language apps teach French, Spanish and Mandarin. Almost nobody builds joyful, story-first learning for Kikuyu, Dholuo, Luganda, Kamba or coastal Swahili. Until now.

Every story on MotherTalk is an act of preservation. When a learner finishes Wanjiru's Market Day, a piece of Kikuyu lives on in a new voice. Multiply that by thousands of learners and dozens of languages, and a generation reconnects with where it comes from.
