Our mission

Across Africa, young people are growing up unable to speak their grandparents' languages. MotherTalk exists to change that. We turn Kikuyu, Kamba, Swahili, Dholuo and Luganda into stories you can live, art you can paint and phrases you can speak out loud, so learning a mother tongue feels like play instead of homework.

Built by Andrew Shedrack Anderson and Anita Mwaura Njuguini.
